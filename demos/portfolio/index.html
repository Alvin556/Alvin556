Reputation Watch

For restaurants, roofers, dentists, and anyone whose next customer reads Google reviews first.

Before

You check your Google rating when you remember to. You find out about a bad review from a friend. You have no idea what competitors are doing.

After
  • Your rating and your competitors' ratings are logged every week.
  • New reviews are tagged by mood and theme: pricing, speed, staff.
  • A reply is drafted for each of your own reviews.
  • A short Monday digest tells you what changed and what to do about it.
